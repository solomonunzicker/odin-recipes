Baked Mac & Cheese


A creamy pasty with three kinds of cheese and macaroni pasta, finished in an oven for a crispy texture.
Another great dish for serving large amounts of people and is great the next day too!

Ingredients

  • 12 ounces elbow macaroni
  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 ½ cups shredded Cheddar cheese
  • 2 tablespoons butter, melted
  • salt and pepper to taste

Step by step instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Lightly grease a 2-quart baking dish.

  2. Boil macaroni in a large pot of lightly salted water until barely cooked, about 5 minutes. Drain and set aside to cool slightly.

  3. Whisk milk and egg together in a large bowl; stir in cheese and butter.

  4. Place par-boiled macaroni in the prepared baking dish. Pour milk mixture over macaroni, season with salt and pepper; stir until combined. Press mixture evenly into the baking dish.

  5. Bake, uncovered, in the preheated oven until golden brown, 30 to 40 minutes.
